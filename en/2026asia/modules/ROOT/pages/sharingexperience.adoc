= Sharing experience

We would like to invite all Node Managers, independently of whether you are able to participate in the meeting, to consider submitting slides on the topics highlighted in the https://docs.gbif.org/nodes-implementation-2026[2026 Nodes Implementation Plan^].

== Template

____
xref:attachment$Node-inputs-Regional-template.pptx[Slides template] (PowerPoint, 36.9 MB)
____

Please use the template to prepare slides to share your experience ahead of the Regional Nodes Meeting. We will share submitted slides for background ahead of the events. Alternatively, you have the option to submit a video presentation, which should ideally be within the range of 3 to 5 minutes.

We are specifically requesting experience sharing relating to the strategic objectives of the 2026 Nodes Implementation Plan:

* https://docs.gbif.org/nodes-implementation-2026/en/#engage-research-communities-for-data-mobilization-and-use[Engage research communities for data mobilization and use^]
* https://docs.gbif.org/nodes-implementation-2026/en/#support-national-and-regional-biodiversity-commitments-and-the-science-policy-interface[Support national and regional biodiversity commitments and the science-policy interface^]
* https://docs.gbif.org/nodes-implementation-2026/en/#promote-open-biodiversity-data-approaches-within-the-business-and-finance-sectors[Promote open biodiversity data approaches within the business and finance sectors^]
* https://docs.gbif.org/nodes-implementation-2026/en/#support-node-development-through-knowledge-sharing-and-mobility-of-skills[Support node development through knowledge sharing and mobility of skills^]
* https://docs.gbif.org/nodes-implementation-2026/en/#develop-capacity-within-regional-communities-of-practice[Develop capacity within regional communities of practice^]
* https://docs.gbif.org/nodes-implementation-2026/en/#contribute-to-data-model-enhancements[Contribute to data model enhancements^]

After you have completed adding your examples to the template, you can then upload them via Box.
